3. Key Findings & Conclusions

Triple ASR is the clear winner. Combining ALL three ASR systems (VibeVoice + Parakeet + Qwen3) yields 4.13 combined (greedy, equal weight), beating VibeVoice-only (3.68 temp05) by +0.45 and Ensemble-only (3.91 greedy) by +0.22. The triple-ASR reconciliation prompt lets MOSS cross-reference three independent transcriptions via majority vote.
Massive YouTube improvement with triple ASR. Triple greedy achieves 4.56 on YT scenes — +0.48 over VibeVoice greedy (4.08) and +0.17 over Ensemble greedy (4.39). Key gains: completeness 4.5 (VV: 3.7), bursts 4.5 (VV: 4.0), quality 4.5 (VV: 3.4).
Triple ASR also improves synthetic scenes. Triple temp05 achieves 3.74 on synthetic, matching VV temp05 (3.70) with +0.04. Unlike Ensemble-only (3.50) which hurt synthetic by -0.20, triple ASR avoids the degradation by including VibeVoice's end-to-end ASR as a third reference.
Three ASR systems compensate for each other's weaknesses. VibeVoice excels at clean TTS with built-in segmentation. Parakeet provides precise word-level timestamps. Qwen3-ASR catches words the others miss. Together, MOSS can cross-validate and pick the best from each — majority vote resolves disagreements, extra words get included.
Ensemble-only (2 ASR) is the worst config. Removing VibeVoice from the mix hurts: Ensemble combined 3.91 greedy / 3.66 temp05 is worse than VibeVoice-only. The 2-ASR reconciliation creates confusion on synthetic TTS (completeness drops from 4.19 to 3.38).
Greedy decoding wins with triple ASR. With triple ASR context, greedy outperforms temp=0.5 on equal-weight combined (4.13 vs 3.99). The richer context from 3 ASR sources reduces the need for sampling diversity. Exception: synthetic scenes slightly prefer temp=0.5 (3.74 vs 3.70).
v2 layered prompt HURTS — aggressive completeness causes hallucination. The v2 prompt instructing exhaustive foreground/background annotation drops combined from 4.13 (v1) to 3.82 (v2 greedy). SFX accuracy collapses (synth: 3.56 → 2.04, −1.52). The model hallucinates room tone, ambient hum, and orchestral underscore in clean TTS scenes. Lesson: 'false positives are less harmful than missing events' is wrong — Gemini heavily penalizes phantom sounds.
Equal weighting reveals stronger triple advantage. With equal domain weighting (Synth + YT) / 2, the triple advantage is more visible: Triple greedy 4.13 vs VV temp05 3.68 (+0.45). Scene-count weighting (old) diluted the YT improvement because synthetic had 5x more scenes.
